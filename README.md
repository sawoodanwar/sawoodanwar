## Research Statement

I am a computational social scientist working at the intersection of **natural language processing**, **social media analysis**, and **computational communication research**. My work investigates how digital platforms mediate collective emotion, public discourse, and information ecosystems during societal crises — with a particular focus on platform-native engagement signals (such as Facebook Reactions) as proxies for affective public response.

Methodologically, I combine **corpus-based text analysis**, **embedding-based topic modelling** (BERTopic, STM), **time-series analysis**, **lexicon-based sentiment analysis**, and **LLM-assisted semantic annotation** to study large-scale social media corpora. I am also experienced in **survey methods**, **psychometric scale validation**, and **experimental designs** for communication research.

## Doctoral Thesis

> **"Facebook Reactions" as Emotional Indicators: A Multi-Method Approach to Analyzing User Engagement with COVID-19 News on Indian Media Platforms**  
> *Sawood Anwar* — University of Urbino Carlo Bo, 2025  
> 🔗 [Institutional Repository (ORA)](https://ora.uniurb.it/handle/11576/2761691)

**Abstract:** This thesis investigates the role of Facebook Reactions as indicators of public sentiment and engagement with COVID-19 pandemic-related news in India during different stages of the pandemic (March 24, 2020 – March 31, 2022). The study employs a mixed-methods approach combining time-series analysis, embedding-based topic modelling, LLM-assisted cluster labelling, and lexicon-based sentiment analysis. The dataset comprises **68,319 Facebook posts** from four major English-language Indian news outlets, with a focused subset of **8,622 posts** covering the early pandemic phase (March 24 – April 14, 2020). Findings reveal how discrete reaction types (Like, Love, Haha, Wow, Sad, Angry) function as affective engagement signals that track shifting public sentiment across dominant news themes and pandemic phases.

### Time-Series & Digital Engagement

#### ⏱️ [timeseries-facebook-engagement-r](https://github.com/sawoodanwar/timeseries-facebook-engagement-r)
`R` `Time-Series` `Anomaly Detection` `Social Media`

### Topic Modelling

#### 🧠 [stm-social-media-r](https://github.com/sawoodanwar/stm-social-media-r)
`R` `Structural Topic Model` `Social Media` `Reproducible Workflow`

Fully reproducible STM (Structural Topic Model) pipeline for social media corpora in R. Covers corpus construction, document-feature matrix (DFM) building, `searchK` model selection, prevalence covariate specification, `estimateEffect` inference, and `ggplot2`-based publication-quality visualisation.

#### 🤖 [bertopic-media-topics](https://github.com/sawoodanwar/bertopic-media-topics)
`Python` `R` `BERTopic` `Sentence Transformers` `Embedding-Based NLP`

BERTopic pipeline for thematic analysis of media corpora: sentence embeddings → UMAP dimensionality reduction → HDBSCAN clustering → c-TF-IDF topic representations → optional LLM-based semantic labelling. Outputs exported to R for post-hoc engagement analysis.
