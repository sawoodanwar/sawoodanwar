### Sawood Anwar

PhD in Humanities (Text and Communication Sciences), University of Urbino Carlo Bo, Italy.

I work at the intersection of **social media analysis**, computational communication research, and NLP. My research focuses on platformed communication, public engagement, disinformation, crisis communication, and digital methods.

## Research Areas
- Social media data analysis
- Computational communication science
- NLP and topic modeling
- Time series analysis
- Sentiment and reaction analysis
- Disinformation and problematic health content
- Crisis communication

## Methods and Tools
- R, Python
- Topic modeling and text embeddings
- Sentiment analysis
- Time-series analysis
- Network analysis with Gephi
- Reproducible research workflows

## Featured Project
- [facebook-reactions-covid19-india](https://github.com/sawoodanwar/facebook-reactions-covid19-india)  
  A public-facing project repository describing methods, structure, and reproducible components from my research on Facebook reactions and COVID-19 news engagement in India. No thesis PDF or restricted data is shared.
